The idea

More than a borehole

Lasting access to water depends on choosing the right source, planning infrastructure carefully, and making sure the system can be maintained over time.

01

Understand the need

Work with residents and local authorities to assess water access, demand, seasonal challenges, and existing services before choosing a solution.

02

Design with experts

Engage qualified water professionals to survey groundwater potential and assess a borehole, solar pumping, storage, distribution points, and livestock access where feasible.

03

Plan for the long term

Develop a community-led management and maintenance approach, with transparent responsibilities and a realistic plan for operating costs.

Proposed pilot

Start small. Learn. Scale responsibly.

The initiative is currently at the idea stage. The pilot would proceed only after local needs, technical feasibility, permissions, and funding are assessed.

1. Community assessment

Confirm needs, beneficiaries, existing water options, and local priorities.

2. Technical feasibility

Commission qualified surveys, water-quality planning, design, and cost estimates.

3. Build partnerships

Coordinate with authorities, engineers, funders, and community representatives.

4. Pilot and evaluate

If feasible and funded, implement, monitor reliability, and document lessons for future villages.

Our long-term vision

A model that can serve more villages

Kwengaje would be the starting point for learning how to deliver and maintain rural water infrastructure in a way that is practical, locally supported, and adaptable.

What we are working toward

• Reliable water access for households and livestock, based on verified local needs.

• Strong community participation and clear maintenance responsibilities.

• A documented approach that can be improved and replicated in other underserved villages.

The population served, water source, project costs, and implementation schedule will be confirmed through community consultation and professional assessment. No construction or funding is claimed at this stage.
